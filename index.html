QUESTION.

A few things you might be curious about.

What makes Diet Coke different from Coca-Cola Zero Sugar?+

Both are sugar-free and calorie-free. Diet Coke has its own distinct, lighter taste, while Coca-Cola Zero Sugar is designed to taste more like Coca-Cola Original Taste.

Does Diet Coke contain caffeine?+

In the US, a 12 fl oz can of Diet Coke Original contains 46 mg of caffeine. Caffeine Free Diet Coke is also available.

When was Diet Coke introduced?+

Diet Coke was introduced in 1982, with its debut announced at a press event in New York City.

Does Diet Coke contain sugar or calories?+

Diet Coke Original has zero sugar and zero calories per 12 fl oz can in the US. Check the package for information specific to your market.
